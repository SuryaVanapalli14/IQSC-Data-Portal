# 👮 Police Forms System - Login Credentials Guide

Welcome to the CCRB & Police Forms administrative workspace. Below are the pre-seeded login credentials for the three permission roles within this system.

---

## 🔑 Primary Administrative & Oversight Accounts

Use these accounts to manage forms, construct dynamic schemas, view real-time compliance matrices, and administer oversight folders:

| Role | Name | Email Address | Password | Privileges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ADMIN** | System Administrator | `admin@mail.com` | `admin` | Full system control, form builder, user manager, logs |
| **CCRB** | CCRB Oversight | `ccrb@mail.com` | `ccrb` | Metrics viewer, submission analytics, read-only oversight |

---

## 🚨 Station Officer Accounts (USER Role)

* **Email:** `station@mail.com`
